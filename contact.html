FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

Do you cut glass to size?
Yes. We can cut glass to your specified dimensions in-store. Include the glass type, thickness and sizes you need when you request a quote.
Do you deliver outside of Kwekwe?
Yes, we service the wider Midlands province including Gweru, Zvishavane, Shurugwi and surrounding areas. Delivery is available — contact us for a delivery quote based on your location and order size.
Do you supply to individual homeowners, or only contractors?
We welcome everyone — homeowners, self-builders, aluminium fabricators and large contractors alike. Walk-in customers are always welcome at our Kwekwe premises during business hours.
Can you source glass products not shown on the website?
Yes. If you need a glass thickness, finish or size that is not shown on the website, send us the specification and we will advise on availability, sourcing options and lead times.
Is there a minimum order quantity for glass?
There is no strict minimum order. We can assist with both small and bulk enquiries, and larger quantities may qualify for better pricing depending on the product line.
Do you accept mobile money (EcoCash / OneMoney) payments?
Yes. We accept cash (ZWL & USD), bank transfer (RTGS), EcoCash, OneMoney and major payment cards. Please confirm with our team at the time of purchase for the most current accepted payment methods.
